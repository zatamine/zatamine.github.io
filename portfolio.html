Selected Work

Case studies — resolved incidents, not just projects

Every engagement below closed with a green status board and a post-mortem the client kept. Details anonymized under NDA.

Legacy → AWS/Zero-Downtime CutoverKubernetes · Terraform

E-commerce platform off a single legacy VM fleet

14 bare-metal hosts, one database cluster, and a 2-hour nightly maintenance window that was the only safe time to change anything. Re-architected as Kubernetes on EKS with blue/green cutover; the switchover itself took 4 minutes — inside the old window.

RESOLVED · 0 min downtime during migration · infra cost down 38% post-rightsizing

Fintech API/Reliability ProgramGo · Prometheus · SLOs

From pager fatigue to a real error budget

60+ pages per week, most from flapping thresholds on metrics nobody had tuned. Introduced SLOs tied to user-facing latency and success rate, rewrote alerting around burn rates, and cut noise by ~85% — while catching two genuine incidents early.

RESOLVED · MTTR down from 41 → 9 min · on-call satisfaction: “we sleep again” (their words)

Data Platform/GCP Migration + GitOpsGKE · Argo CD

Migrating a batch-heavy data platform to GKE without missing a run

A nightly ETL workload that could not skip even one cycle. Built parallel-run validation with row-level checksums, migrated 12 job families over six weeks with automated rollback, and left a fully GitOps-managed cluster the team now runs solo.

RESOLVED · 100% of nightly runs delivered during migration window · handoff in 3 weeks
