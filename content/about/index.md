I'm **Amine** — an independent software engineer and SRE consultant working with teams that need systems which are fast under load and boring to operate.

## What I care about

- **Throughput without drama.** High-concurrency Go services where p99 stays flat as traffic climbs, not degrades gracefully into a war room.
- **Resilience by design.** SLOs tied to user impact, observability that tells you *why* something is wrong before the pager does, and chaos tests that fail in staging — never on call at 3am.
- **Migrations nobody has to tweet about.** Zero-downtime database and Kubernetes cutovers where the rollback plan is written *before* the cutover window opens.

## How I work

I embed with your team (or take a project end-to-end) and leave behind working systems, runbooks, dashboards and people who understand them. No black boxes: everything I touch is Terraform-managed, documented and boring in production.

## Background

Years of building and operating high-traffic backend services on AWS and on-prem — Go, Kubernetes, Prometheus/Grafana/OpenTelemetry observability stacks, Terraform-based infrastructure, and the live traffic migrations that connect them all. The [blog](/posts/) is where I write about what I've learned.
